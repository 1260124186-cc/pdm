`pdm venv purge` now reconciles central virtualenvs against the ownership marker written at creation time and only removes environments whose owner project no longer exists. Active, manually created and unconfirmed entries are retained, the full audit is printed before deletion, the command is safe to re-run after an interruption, and a stale `.pdm-python` pointing at a removed environment is cleared. Use `pdm venv purge --all` for the previous blanket-purge behavior.
